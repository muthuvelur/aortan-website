Welcome to AORTAN

AORTAN brings together Tamil families from Tamil Nadu now living across the UK. We organise cultural celebrations, festivals and community get-togethers so that our traditions, language and values stay alive for our children and grandchildren growing up here.

What We Do

  • Annual Pongal / Thamizhar Thirunal celebration with dance, music and drama
  • Support for newly arrived Tamil families settling in the UK
  • Tamil language and culture resources for children
  • A friendly network for the wider Tamil Nadu community across the region
