Past Events

Thamizhar Thirunal – Pongal 2026

Walsall Football Club, Jimmy Walker Suite — January 2026

Our 2026 Pongal celebration brought members together for an evening of cultural performances — dance, vocal and instrumental items from our young and senior members — followed by a musical drama night, dinner and dancing. Thank you to everyone who performed, volunteered and joined us on the night.

Previous Years

AORTAN has celebrated Pongal / Thamizhar Thirunal every year since 2009, along with occasional smaller cultural evenings and community meet-ups. Photos from past celebrations are on our Photos & Videos page.
